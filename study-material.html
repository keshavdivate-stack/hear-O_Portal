Once the patient is registered and logged into the HearO app, walk them through each step below in order.

  1. 1

    Complete initial device setup

    Guide the patient through granting HealthKit/device permissions and recording their first baseline reading in the app. This step is required before the app will start tracking daily readings.

    What the patient sees in the app
    1 2 3 4 5 6

    "Please find a quiet and comfortable place to sit before we begin"

    "Hold the phone in a way that doesn't block the microphone"

    "Keep the phone at a comfortable distance - not too close, not too far"

    "Get ready to record by reading the sentence on the screen. Press the blue button to begin recording"

    "Press the green button when you finish speaking"

    "If the recording isn't clear enough, you may be asked to record again"

    Then a practice run, before the real recording

    "Now let's try to record together" -- patient taps Let's practice

    Reads a sample sentence aloud, then taps the green button to stop

    If audio is unclear: "Let's try again" -- ask the patient to speak clearly in a quiet spot

    Once it sounds clear, patient taps Next to start the real recording

  2. 2

    Confirm daily check-in & support contact

    Make sure the patient knows how to complete their daily check-in in the app, and how to reach the care team through in-app Chat if they run into any questions or issues.

  3. 3

    Confirm onboarding is complete

    Once the patient begins sending readings, their status automatically moves from Registered to Baseline on Patient List -- confirm this has happened before ending the onboarding session.
