Color Guide

A simple guide to the colors used, and what each one means.

1. Primary & Secondary Colors

These are the main colors used across the app for buttons, links, and highlights.

Primary Color — Blue

Blue is our main color. It is used for links, buttons, and important actions.

Blue#2AA9E0

Secondary Colors

These extra colors are used for special tags and labels, mostly in the clinic admin screens. Active Status Blue is the text color for Open and Active states on tickets and incidents (for example an Open ticket or an Active incident).

Button Blue#2563EB
Active Status Blue#0E5C99

Neutral Colors

Grays are used for backgrounds, borders, and regular text that isn't a color status.

Page Background#F4F7FA
Border Gray#E7EAEE
Body Text#57626D
Mid Gray#9AA5B1
Dark Text#1B2430

2. Semantic Colors

Semantic colors have a fixed meaning everywhere in the app. Green always means "good," orange always means "warning," maroon always means "error, critical or escalated," red always means "patient priority," and so on. Each color has three versions: a solid color (for icons and dots), a text color (easier to read), and a light background (for badges).

Green — Good / Success

Green means everything is fine: data is complete, a task is done, or the answer is "Yes."

Green#3FBE84
Green Text#15803D
Green Background#EAFBF3

Orange — Warning

Orange means something needs attention: data is low, a dose was missed, the answer is "No," or a task is still in progress.

Orange#F2994A
Orange Text#C25E00
Orange Background#FEEDDC

Maroon — Error / Critical / Escalated

Maroon means something is wrong or urgent on the system side: a system error, a failed action, invalid input, a Critical severity, or an Escalated ticket or incident. It's a deep, muted red — distinct from Priority Red and from the Orange warning tone — so it never gets mistaken for a patient's Priority status or a simple warning.

Maroon#9F1C3B
Maroon Text#7A0F2B
Maroon Background#FCE8EC

Red — Patient Priority

Red is reserved for patient Priority status only. It is not used for system errors, Critical severity or Escalated items — those use maroon.

Red#F16C6C
Red Text#D92D20
Red Background#FDEDED

Yellow — Medium Caution

Yellow sits between orange and gray. It marks things that are medium priority — not urgent, but not fine either.

Yellow#D9A404
Yellow Text#CA8A04
Yellow Background#FFFBE8

Gray — Disabled / Not Available

Gray means something is turned off, not filled in, or not clickable.

Disabled Border#E7EAEE
Disabled Text#8D929A
Disabled Input Text#9AA5B1

3. Badges & Status Pills

Badges and status pills are the small rounded labels that show a patient's or task's status at a glance. They use a light background color with matching darker text from the semantic colors above.

Active / Completed / Resolved Open / Active (Tickets & Incidents) Pending / In Progress Patient Priority Critical Escalated Error Medium Priority Low Priority / Archived
ColorStatusWhat it means
Active / Completed / ResolvedGreen background, green text. Everything is good — e.g. an Active patient, user or organization.
Open / Active (Tickets & Incidents)Light blue background, Active Status Blue text. A ticket or incident that is open and being worked on.
Pending / In ProgressOrange-tinted background, orange text. Waiting or needs action.
Patient PriorityRed background, red text. A patient in Priority status. Red is used for this only.
CriticalMaroon background, maroon text. The highest severity/priority on a ticket, incident or rule.
EscalatedMaroon background, maroon text. Sent to a higher level — treated as urgent, same as Critical.
ErrorMaroon background, maroon text. A system error or failed action.
Medium PriorityYellow background, yellow text. Somewhat important.
Low Priority / ArchivedLight gray background, gray text. Not urgent.
Quick rule of thumb: green = good, blue = open/active ticket or incident, orange = warning or in progress, maroon = error, critical or escalated, red = patient priority only, yellow = medium, gray = low or inactive.
