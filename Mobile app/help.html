Help & Support

When should I record?

Every morning, immediately after waking up and before breakfast, for the most consistent results.

What if HearO can't hear me clearly?

Find a quiet room, hold the phone so the microphone isn't blocked, and speak at a normal pace and volume.

Can I add my own health measurements?

Yes. Go to My Measurements from the home screen and tap the + button to add a new blood pressure reading.

Who can see my recordings?

Only you and the clinical team connected to your account can view your recordings and measurements.
