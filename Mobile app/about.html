About HearO

Version 1.0.0

HearO is a voice-based monitoring app developed by Cordio Medical that helps you and your clinical team track changes in your health over time.

Each morning, HearO asks you to record a short sentence. The recording is analyzed to help detect early changes related to your condition, so your care team can respond sooner.

Your recordings and measurements are kept private and are only shared with your connected clinic.
